About the series

A workshop where PhD students and postdoctoral researchers in applied microeconomics present their work to an audience of their peers and of senior researchers, and leave with something useful to do next.

Early-career researchers rarely get an unhurried hour on their own paper. This workshop is built around that: every presentation is assigned a discussant, normally a professor, so that each paper receives constructive feedback from a senior researcher rather than a polite round of questions. A poster session gives further submissions a place on the programme, and the meeting is deliberately small enough that participants can follow each other's work across the two days.

The series is hosted at the Faculty of Business and Economics (HEC Lausanne), on the shore of Lake Geneva, and is open to applied economists of any field — labour, public, political economy, development, health, education, and the economics of firms and innovation among them. Participation is free, and the organisers cover meals and, for participants affiliated outside Switzerland, accommodation.
